What this project is about

OSINT (Open-Source Intelligence) means collecting information that is already public, without sending anything to the target. Think of it as reading a company's public notice board before deciding how it could be attacked, and how to protect it.

GOAL

Map the footprint

Find owner, hosting, DNS, subdomains and history of the domain.

RULE

Stay passive

No port scans, no logins, no exploits. Only public databases and search engines.

OUTPUT

Risk and fixes

Rate each finding, then say how a real company would reduce it.

About the target: Terralogic is a real technology services company (software, cloud, cybersecurity and IT services, founded 2008, more than 10 offices, per its own About page). Because it is a real organization, this project stays strictly passive: public WHOIS, DNS, search engines and archives only. Do not port scan, probe or log in to terralogic.com or any of its subdomains. Confirm with your instructor that this target is approved for your coursework.

What an attacker could do with this

Phishing

Fake renewal or transfer emails using the registrar and company name.

Targeted attacks

Pick exploits that match the technology and hosting provider.

Forgotten sites

Hunt old test subdomains that nobody patches.

Domain takeover

Grab the domain if it expires and is not renewed.

Recommendations

1

Keep privacy and lock

WHOIS privacy and registrar transfer lock stay on.

2

Auto-renew and MFA

Protect the registrar account so the domain cannot be lost or hijacked.

3

Subdomain inventory

List everything. Remove or protect test and legacy sites.

4

Hide the non-public

Use noindex, login or VPN for internal pages.

5

Hide versions

Remove software versions from banners and error pages.

6

Email DNS records

Publish SPF, DKIM and DMARC to block spoofing.

7

Watch certificates

Monitor certificate transparency logs for unexpected subdomains.
