Good controls observed

  • WHOIS privacy and redacted contacts stop harvesting of names and emails.
  • Four registrar locks reduce the risk of hijacking or unauthorised transfer.
  • Five managed name servers give DNS redundancy.
  • Domain paid ahead to 2029 lowers the risk of expiry.

Limitations and conclusion

  • Internal scan: top 1000 TCP ports of one host; no UDP, no full-port scan, no scripts, no exploitation.
  • External findings are passive only; other tools may find different subdomains, and two items come from a 2019 snapshot.
  • OS and versions come from fingerprints and banners and may be inaccurate.
  • Validation items 8 and 9 are still pending.

From the outside, terralogic.com exposes moderate but manageable information, offset by good controls. From the inside, the lab host exposes 23 services including a reported root shell and several clear-text protocols. The most urgent fixes are removing the bind shell, retiring Telnet and the r-services, and protecting the staging site. The AI assistant sped up classification and drafting, but checking its output showed where wording needed care, so AI results must be verified before they go into a security report.
